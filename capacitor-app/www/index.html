Why Choose Us

Built Different. Built Better.

Our fly ash bricks deliver superior strength, precision dimensions, and environmental benefits that set us apart from traditional brick manufacturers.

🧱

Superior Strength

Compressive strength of 7.5 N/mm² and above — exceeding IS 12894 standards. Every batch tested rigorously.

🌿

Eco-Friendly

Made from fly ash — a thermal power plant byproduct — reducing landfill waste and lowering carbon footprint.

📐

Precise Dimensions

Machine-manufactured to exact sizes, ensuring uniform thickness and reducing mortar consumption by up to 30%.

🔥

Thermal Insulation

Excellent thermal insulation keeps buildings cooler in summer and warmer in winter, reducing energy costs.

💧

Low Water Absorption

Only 8–12% water absorption — significantly lower than red clay bricks — making structures durable in monsoon.

🚚

Bulk Delivery

We handle bulk orders with on-time delivery across Raigarh, Kharsia, and all of Chhattisgarh.

Our Story

Passion for Quality

Founded by Dev Kumar Dansena, DEV Fly Ash Bricks began with a simple mission: to produce the highest quality fly ash bricks while making construction more sustainable.

Under his leadership, DEV Fly Ash Bricks has grown from a small unit to a large-scale manufacturing operation serving hundreds of projects across Chhattisgarh.
